The platform in detail

Three parts, one integrated solution

Technician app, customer app and web dashboard — all in your brand, designed for day-to-day pool maintenance.

1. Customer App

The app your end customer uses on their smartphone: service history, reports and water parameters, bookings and notifications. All in your brand — transparency that builds trust and retention.

  • Direct upsell to the customer: offers for additional products and services appear in the app; the customer buys with one tap — more recurring revenue for your company.
  • Customer loyalty: history, reports and water parameters in near real time; the customer sees the value of the service and is more likely to stay.
  • Reinforce your brand: the app is 100% in your identity; every use reinforces your company as the go-to for pool care.
  • Less work for your team: fewer calls and repeated questions; the customer has the info in their pocket and booking notifications reduce no-shows.
  • Available on Android and iOS, in your brand: a professional experience across the whole customer ecosystem.

2. Technician App

The app your technician uses in the field: day schedule, customer and pool records, water parameters and photos, digital signature and automatic confirmation to the customer. All offline-capable for areas with weak signal.

  • Upsell at point of service: the technician sees segmented offers (products, treatments, extra services) and can close on the spot — more revenue per visit.
  • Simplified processes: one tool for schedule, records, parameters and photos; less paper and less rework in admin.
  • Reinforce your brand: the technician works with your app and your identity; the customer associates the service with your company, not a third party.
  • Loyalty through transparency: report and digital signature on site; the customer gets immediate confirmation and feels secure — fewer cancellations.
  • Stable operation: works offline and syncs later; routes and data always to hand, even in areas with weak signal.

3. Web Dashboard (Admin)

The web interface for managing your company: customers, pools, technicians, scheduled and completed services, reports and upsell campaigns. 360° view for more revenue, retention and lean processes.

  • Upsell under control: create segmented campaigns and offers (by customer, pool or profile); track performance and adjust to maximise additional revenue.
  • Loyalty and visibility: see retention, satisfaction and app usage metrics; identify at-risk customers and strengthen the relationship.
  • Process simplification: centralised management of customers, pools and technicians; single calendar, automatic reports and less manual admin.
  • Reinforce your brand: everything the customer and technician see is from your brand; you control the experience and company image.
  • Data-driven decisions: performance reports, schedule compliance and campaign results to scale the business sustainably.
